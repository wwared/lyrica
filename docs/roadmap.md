# Roadmap

Work proceeds bottom-up in small steps, each usable on its own. Design details live in `architecture.md`.

## Current state

Now playing, the live queue, stored playlists, the album button, the toolbar's player toggles and search mode are done and checked on phones, except the volume pill, the queue marquees, the toolbar's even spacing, the search field's clear button and the query language, not yet tried by hand.

- **Foundation:** Nix flake (dev shell, build, fmt, check, NixOS module); `lyrica-mpd` with the commands lyrica needs; server bridging WebSocket clients to MPD (idle, command and queue connections, reconnect with backoff, offline state, stored playlist listing and loading, album start, queue search); frontend embedded in the binary.
- **Now playing:** title, artist (album artist as fallback), album, album art from the HTTP mirror, smooth progress bar with the time beside it, tap / drag seeking on the bar (full mode), previous / play-pause / next, random / single / repeat, MPD volume in a vertical pill growing up from its toggle, bouncing marquee for long lines, fixed-height layout pinned to the bottom, animated full / mini mode switch.
- **Live queue (mini mode):** the whole queue in a virtual list of unbounded length under the toolbar; rows (track number, title, artist · album, duration) on alternating backgrounds, long titles and artist · album lines bouncing in marquees while on screen, loaded on demand while shown, two lists ahead on each side; a separator at each album change and the album's cover beside its top visible row, pinned while the album scrolls, probed two lists ahead, also while scrolling; centred on the current song when opened, on each song change (paused for 15 s after the user scrolls) and on a tap on now playing's cover; the current song is highlighted; the visible rows keep their old text across a queue change until their refetch lands; tapping a row plays it. Nothing loads for it in full mode.
- **Stored playlists:** a toolbar button lists them afresh on each tap (a toast when there are none or listing failed) in a modal; "Load" replaces the queue, with a modal "Loading…" box (seen only after 300 ms) until MPD is done, then the list at its top, or MPD's error in a small modal.
- **Album from the start:** a toolbar button, its last, plays the current song's album from its first entry with random and single off (repeat and the queue untouched) and centres the list on it.
- **Search mode:** a toolbar button turns the toolbar into a search field with a clear button; the query language (words, quoted text, `^`, `-`, `/regex/`, `field:`) finds matches in the queue, the first after the current song centred, ▲▼ in a pill over the list's bottom step through them (wrapping), with their position and count, matches marked in the list; auto-centring held meanwhile; leaving by ✕, Escape or full mode.
- **Player toggles in mini mode:** play here, volume, random, single and repeat, in the toolbar's evenly spaced row between the playlists and album buttons; the volume pill grows down from there, the same size as in full mode.
- **Play here:** plays MPD's `httpd` stream in the browser, with title, artist, album and cover on the lock screen.
- **iOS fit:** fixed viewport (no bounce, no zoom, no selection), full height in home-screen apps, safe areas, content below the system fade at the top of home-screen apps.
- **Dark only:** gruvbox dark with its accent colours, regardless of the system setting.
- **PWA:** app icon and favicon, screen kept on while visible, portrait on Android.

## Open work

- **Search** (design in `architecture.md` → "Search"): find matches in the queue and step through them, centred, without playing them. Each step lands as its own commits, with tests, measurements and docs.
  1. **Toolbar row** (done): one evenly spaced row of 44 px buttons (playlists, toggles, album); positions checked at 390 and 375 px, with and without play here.
  2. **`lyrica-mpd` search** (done): `search_queue` (`status` + `playlistsearch FILTER window START:END` in one command list) returns the queue version, length and the matches' positions; `Filter` renders MPD's filter syntax with its quoting. Timings on the real MPD under "Queue measurements" → "Queue search".
  3. **Query, phase 1** (done): `query.rs` → `compile` splits plain words on Unicode whitespace (no nom yet: phase 2's grammar needs it) and compiles each to a `(*UTF)` literal regex over the default fields (file first, `OR` as `!(… !~ … AND …)`), longest word first, as a `lyrica_mpd::Filter`; a blank query is an error. Checked against the real MPD under "Queue measurements" → "Queue search".
  4. **Server search** (done): `search.rs` and the queue task: `search` / `matches` / `searchfailed` / `searchstale` / `searchend`; chunk 0 at once, then whole chunks bracketing the first match after `from` (interpolated, bisecting after a read that doesn't halve the bracket; read whole since a probe costs the scan to its last match), every chunk read sent, the last with `first`; chunk fetches like windows; waiting requests before each step, searches taking turns, 2 ms between steps so state pushes wait out at most one scan; stale on a version change. Over the WS under "Queue measurements" → "Queue search" → "Server search": first match in 17–158 ms over 1–5 chunks, every step ≤ 86 ms; under back-to-back searches windows ≤ 92 ms and state pushes ≤ 60 ms.
  5. **Search mode UI** (done): `search.ts` (Vitest) holds the state as pure transitions, `search-mode.tsx` applies them (requests, toasts, the list through `QueueStore`: `search(view)`, `reveal(position)`) and renders the search bar and the pill; the toast lifted to `App`; matched rows `bg1` with a yellow edge, the focused one `bg2` and yellow; 64 px of room after the last row; stale on another queue version, `searchstale` or a closed socket, rerun by the next ▲▼. Under "Measurements" → "Search mode": ▲▼ within held chunks 1–7 ms to the DOM, a chunk fetch 33 ms, state pushes still ≤ 1 ms after MPD's `idle` with no list mutation, flings with every row marked keep every frame.
  6. **Count** (done): `Count` in `search.rs`, a `Bracket` with `from = length − 1` fed every chunk the search reads, finds the last chunk after the first match, then `{"type":"total","id","version","total"}`; count reads wait until the queue task has been idle 100 ms, so the rows around the match load first; `searchstale` if the queue changes meanwhile. Client: `total` sets the pill's total, `searchstale` after `first` marks the matches stale. Totals 85–880 ms after the request, 0–9 reads of ≤ 84 ms each, all checked; rows still fill 24–32 ms after the first match; with searches and counts back to back and fling-like window bursts, windows max 68 ms and state pushes max 70 ms ("Queue measurements" → "Server search" → "Count"; "Measurements" → "Search count").
  7. **iPhone check (phase 1)** (done): the whole checklist as designed (keyboard, no zoom, no page shift, pill reach and placement, count, stepping, styles, scrolling, playback, toasts, leaving, rerun after unlock); "Measurements" → "Search mode by hand". It asked for a clear button in the field, added (Chromium-checked).
  8. **Query, phase 2** (done): `query.rs` parses the whole grammar with nom into terms (negated, field or default fields, contains / starts with / regex), then compiles them: positive literals longest first, then positive regexes, then negated terms; user regexes behind `(*UTF)(*UCP)(*LIMIT_MATCH=10000)`; `Missing closing "` / `Missing closing /` errors. Tests for every rule, fallback, escape, error, the ordering and the docs' example table, plus every query of up to 5 pieces from `- ^ " / \ : a é artist:` and two spaces parsing or failing cleanly. On the real MPD ("Queue measurements" → "Queue search" → "Through phase 2 `compile`"): 22 shapes' positions checked against the queue's first 3,000 entries and by tags; one negated term ≤ 84 ms per step; `(*UCP)` up to 1.5× on repeated classes (`\w+ö\w*` 101 → 145 ms a scan). Over the 100 ms ceiling, for step 9: `any:` terms, 105–150 ms per step (`any:love` total 0.4–0.6 s, `any:x` ~1 s); two or more negated terms without a rarer positive one, count reads 136–200 ms (`-live -demo -remix` 8 steps, total 1.3 s). On a throwaway MPD: nested-repeat slips search in 9–27 ms per 1,000 entries but lose matches (negated, keep entries they should drop); a user's own `(*LIMIT_MATCH=n)` after ours wins, so it can lift the limit.
  9. **iPhone check (phase 2)**, by hand in the home-screen app, after deploying the step 8 commit or later (the user; results under "Measurements" by hand, issues fixed or turned into backlog items):
     - **Typing:** whether the keyboard gives straight `"` and `'` and a plain `-` (iOS Smart Punctuation turns them into “ ” ’ and dashes, which the language treats as text); `/`, `^`, `:` reachable.
     - **Shapes:** `radiohead creep`, `"no surprises"`, `^the`, `-live`, `artist:radiohead -album:live`, `title:"it's"`, `file:/\.flac$/`, `/(?-i)LOVE/` (capitals only), `/é\b/`, `AC/DC`, `re:zero` (plain text); each as expected, the count right.
     - **Errors as toasts:** `"abc` (`Missing closing "`), `/abc` (`Missing closing /`), `""` (`Empty search`), `/(/` (MPD's "Error in regex at offset 33: …", the offset counting our 32-character prefix: keep, strip the offset or rewrite it).
     - **Costly shapes**, each while watching the list, a scroll and play / pause: `any:love`, `any:x`, `-live -demo -remix`, `-zzqx -qxzz -xzqz`. Keep, reject, simplify or remove each (ceiling 100 ms per command, slower kept only if worth it).
     - **Regex limit:** decide on a user's own `(*LIMIT_MATCH=n)` lifting ours (strip it, reject it or allow it); never try a large one on the real queue (minutes of blocked MPD). Known misses, if wanted by hand: `(.*e){8}`, `(\w+\s?)+remix`.
     - **Clear button** ⌫: empties the field, the keyboard stays up, the matches stay.
     - Then the query language's reference in the docs updated to what shipped.
